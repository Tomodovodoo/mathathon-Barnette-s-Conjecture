# A writing method for the Barnette exposition

These are HUNCH editorial prescriptions derived from the documented rewrite. Test them by asking a reader to identify the purpose of each construction before reading its proof. They are not new mathematical results. Examples marked LIT refer to O, OpenAI, *Paired states and Hamiltonian cycles in cubic bipartite planar graphs*, September 24, 2026, supplied PDF; or L, Zhentao Li, *A note on the proof of Barnette's conjecture*, arXiv:2610.30490v1, October 21, 2026. C means the supplied *A constructive proof of Barnette's conjecture*, October 18, 2026; its additional assertions remain HUNCH pending a separate proof audit and publication identification. Section and result numbers below identify exact sources.

## A proposed opening and abstract

HUNCH editorial draft, using the LIT route of O §§2–7:

> A Hamiltonian cycle must give degree two at every vertex and connect all vertices. In a cubic graph the complement of a perfect matching satisfies the degree condition, but it may consist of several cycles. We enforce connectivity by constructing a forest in the planar dual. Its edges select one side of each triangular face, so the complementary dual edges form a connected spanning subgraph of degree two.

The first paragraph still owes the forest construction. It should immediately ask how to find that forest, rather than define all later auxiliary graphs.

> We explain the matching proof that every finite simple cubic bipartite 3-connected planar graph has a Hamiltonian cycle avoiding any prescribed edge. In the dual triangulation, the task is to find an acyclic selection with one edge in every face. We construct the selection from two disjoint incidence matchings. A disk identity makes cyclic configurations cancel, while a second grouping of the same weighted sum proves that a forest configuration survives. Plane duality recovers the cycle, and a prescribed facial pattern lets the construction pass across separating triangles.

This is an exposition abstract, not an announcement of independent discovery. If the main theorem includes C's algorithmic claim, the abstract and outline must retain its separate proof obligations. Do not add that claim to this draft by changing one sentence.

## The audit sequence

First freeze the theorem and selected source route. Work backward from a Hamiltonian cycle to degree two and connectivity, then to the selected forest, forest state, cancellation, initial pair and gluing prescription. List each obligation with its scope and exact support. The small worked audit in `DEPENDENCY-AUDIT.md` shows the format; it does not certify all three papers.

Next edit the route, then the notation, then the sentences. Finally check citations and rendered placement. A terminology audit should check mathematical meaning, as the floating reviewers did for the chord parameter, not merely enforce a preferred vocabulary. A figure audit should verify both the plotted/drawn object and its surrounding claim. A final comparison should distinguish a proposed change from an applied one, as the floating title history requires.
