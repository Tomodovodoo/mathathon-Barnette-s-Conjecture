# Proof from memory

Written after looking at all eighteen PDF pages rendered by PyMuPDF at 130 dpi, before opening the TeX, source papers, repository README, or prior audits. This is a record of my reading, not an independent proof. Status of the recalled assertions is LIT, manuscript under audit, pending verification.

The dual of the cubic bipartite plane graph is a simple sphere triangulation with black and white faces. If there are k faces of either colour, there are 3k edges and k+2 vertices. A selection takes one side in every face. If it is acyclic, its dual complement is connected and degree two, hence Hamiltonian. The selection itself has two components, both induced trees. Prescribing one selected dual edge prescribes the avoided primal edge.

Root at a black triangle t0 with corners a,b,c and prescribe ab. Other black faces A and other vertices X both have size n=k-1. A state bijectively assigns each face of A one incident vertex of X. Its opposite sides Q have n edges. Good means Q is a forest with one root per component. Add ac and bc to Q to get a spanning tree S. The dual complement of S is a tree Y on faces, with degree two at every nonroot black face and degree one at t0. Root Y at t0 and select each black face's side towards its child. This gives a selection containing ab.

To exclude cycles in that selection, orient its nonroot sides out of the chosen state corners. Outdegree is one off the roots and zero at roots. Any cycle is directed. For such a cycle, let h be its length, I the number of interior vertices, B,W the interior black and white faces, and ell the boundary sides with black face inside. Euler and signed face counts give B+W=2I+h-2 and 3(B-W)=2ell-h. Bijectivity of the state gives B=I+ell. Hence W=B+1 and h=2ell+3. A selection along the cycle would instead pair interior faces to give W-B=h-2ell=3, a contradiction. The remaining forest has one root per tree, so adding ab creates no cycle.

A pair is two states r,s differing everywhere. Orient Q_s from r to the third corner. This makes goodness equivalent to acyclicity; a good state has exactly one partner. The edges r(t) to s(t) form disjoint directed cycles covering X. There are two reversals, one reversing a Q_s cycle while keeping s fixed, the other swapping r,s on a D cycle. Local signs delta are + when the black face is on the left, otherwise -. Their product epsilon flips under either reversal because the disk count makes each cycle odd. For c cycles, c+ counterclockwise, epsilon=(-1)^((n+c)/2)(-1)^c+, and n and c have the same parity.

The first reversal cancels all partners of each nongood s. For each white face fix a simple face path to t0. At a black face record +1 or -1 if the path turns around its chosen corner, depending on left or right. Summing gives a height and then a total weight omega. Reversing a clockwise D cycle raises each interior white height by one and leaves the others fixed. Its weight is therefore its positive number of enclosed white faces. Group pairs by undirected D. Each class consists of all 2^c orientations. If m is the minimum cycle count, the signed mth power sum of weights kills classes with c>m. Each c=m class contributes the same sign times m! times its positive cycle weights. Thus the total is nonzero, so a good state exists whenever a pair does.

A pair exists without separating triangles by integral max flow on face-vertex incidences with capacities 2,1,2. The cut inequality becomes e(U)-b(U)<=2|U|-4 for root-containing U. Componentwise planar Euler counting proves it. Triangular faces of an induced component are actual faces because there is no separating triangle. Signed black-white balances in larger faces control their deficiency from length four. For separating triangles, cap each disk with the opposite colour. Both smaller pieces are valid triangulations. Solve the piece containing t0, then solve the other prescribing the side selected on the cap. Their selections glue and remain acyclic by the induced-tree property. This completes induction and the return to G.
