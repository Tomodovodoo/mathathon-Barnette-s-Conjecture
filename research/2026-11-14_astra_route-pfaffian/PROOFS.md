# Proofs

All PROVED-HERE statements are T2, unreviewed. Computation is supporting T4 evidence, not a premise in the general proofs.

## 1. A Pfaffian sign is not a connectivity sign

PROVED-HERE. Fix ordered bipartition classes A,B of size n and signs k_ab on edges. For a perfect matching s:A->B put tau(s)=sgn(s) product_a k_a,s(a). A Pfaffian signing makes tau constant. Consequently the coefficient of each matching monomial in det K has the same sign. In a cubic graph Q_s=G-s is a spanning 2-factor, so every Q_s has a cycle. A polynomial with these coefficients cannot cancel just the matchings with disconnected Q_s: their monomials are distinct and their coefficients are nonzero. Even after all edge variables become 1, their contributions have the same sign. This proves failure of that proposed cancellation, not impossibility of a different sign or an enlarged state space.

PROVED-HERE. The linear equations used by `experiment.py` certify the signing. Assign x_e in F2 to each edge and impose sum_{e in s} x_e=parity(s) for every perfect matching s. A solution makes every determinant term +1. For a conformal cycle of length 2k, fix a matching off the cycle and compare its two alternating extensions. Equal determinant terms imply product of signs on the cycle is (-1)^(k-1). Direct an edge A to B for positive sign and B to A for negative sign. Along either traversal the number of forward edges has parity k+sum_C x_e=1. Thus the output signing gives a Pfaffian orientation, checked against every matching rather than inferred from the graph name. Conversely a Pfaffian orientation aligns terms by flipping alternating cycles in the symmetric difference of two matchings. Global common sign can be normalized by flipping every edge at one vertex, so fixing the common sign to +1 loses no solutions.

## 2. Surface cardinality and forest obstruction

PROVED-HERE. Let G be cubic bipartite with 2k vertices, cellularly embedded on an orientable genus-g surface, with three distinct incident faces at each vertex. Euler gives F=k+2-2g. The planar rooted state definition deletes one black vertex and its three incident face labels. Its domain has k-1 elements and codomain k-1-2g. For g>0 there is no bijection. This fails before a disk identity or Pfaffian sign can be used.

PROVED-HERE. On a torus, retaining all k black vertices and all k face labels restores equal cardinality. At each black vertex b, a state s selects one of the three face labels. Q_s joins the other two labels by the edge opposite the selected corner, retaining edge identity. There are k selected links on k vertices. A forest has at most k-1 edges. Hence every root-free Q_s is cyclic. If an odd-Q-cycle sign-reversing involution cancels every cyclic Q_s, the entire state polynomial vanishes. A nonvanishing proof with that same cancellation and that same target is impossible. This is independent of the particular embedding or graph, whenever these definitions are valid.

PROVED-HERE. No edge weighting on a closed oriented cellular surface has strictly positive circulation around every consistently oriented face. Each edge occurs twice with opposite directions, so the sum of all face circulations is zero. Removing an outer face on a sphere avoids this obstruction; declaring a face outer on a torus does not repair the state cardinality above.

## 3. Correct boundary signatures for a cubic trisum

Definition. Label the shared cycle 01230. In a summand remove its four cycle edges. A spanning path system covers every vertex outside the four terminals, has degree two at those interior vertices, and uses each terminal with degree zero or one. The six signatures are S01,S12,S23,S30, a single path between the named adjacent terminals, and T01|23,T03|12, two paths pairing all four terminals. Unused terminals are retained as isolated vertices. These signatures record path connectivity, not just a chosen set of cycle edges.

LIT. GSW Theorems 4.17 and 4.18: a bipartite graph with a perfect matching is Pfaffian iff it has no conformal bisubdivision of K3,3; in a brace, a four-cycle has a conformal cross iff it lies in a conformal bisubdivision of K3,3. A cross joins opposite terminal pairs by internally disjoint paths outside C, with conformal union with C.

PROVED-HERE, using these named results. In a cubic Pfaffian brace which is P4-Hamiltonian, all six signatures occur. Prescribe C minus edge ij. A containing Hamiltonian cycle uses precisely those three C edges, because it cannot contain C itself. Removing them leaves a spanning path with endpoints i,j, giving Sij. To force the C intersection to be matching {01,23}, prescribe the three-edge path made of the outside edge at 0, edge 01, and the outside edge at 1. Cubicity excludes 03 and 12, and forces 23. Removing C leaves two paths spanning the interior. Their pairing cannot be {01,23}, which would close two separate cycles when the C matching is restored. It cannot be {02,13}: that would be a spanning conformal cross, forbidden by 4.17-4.18. Hence the pairing is {03,12}. Interchanging the two C matchings gives the other T signature. All prescribed paths are simple: the outside neighbors at adjacent terminals lie in opposite bipartition classes and are outside C.

PROVED-HERE. Three pieces realizing all six signatures have a Hamiltonian cubic trisum. Choose S01 in piece 1, T03|12 in piece 2, S23 in piece 3. At every terminal precisely two paths meet. Contracting the four paths gives the cycle 0-1-2-3-0. Their interiors are disjoint and cover every interior vertex in all three pieces. Expanding the paths gives a single spanning cycle. This also explains the twelve ordered compatible triples in `data/patterns.json`: choose which piece has two paths, choose one of its two pairings, and assign the two complementary adjacent single paths to the other two pieces in either order, giving 3*2*2=12.

PROVED-HERE, limitation. This lemma proves Hamiltonicity of one trisum given full signatures of its inputs. It does not prove that the result has full signatures at every other four-cycle, nor that a specified P4 can be retained. Therefore it is not by itself a recursive replacement for GSW Lemma 4.12. Carrying P4-Hamiltonicity through recursion supplies precisely that missing stronger invariant.

## 4. Faces retained from pieces are not faces of the trisum

PROVED-HERE. Three cubes sharing a facial square have sixteen vertices after identification. Delete the shared four edges to obtain the cubic trisum. In each cube four of the five remaining facial squares use an edge of the deleted square. Each of those purported faces is now an open path, not a cycle. Keeping all fifteen piece labels also gives fifteen labels against eight black vertices, instead of the required planar ten faces against eight black vertices. Even if one ignores the lost edges and keeps abstract labels, a shared vertex is incident with six retained piece faces, not three: each of the three pieces supplies its two side faces. Thus there is no unique opposite link at that vertex. The proposed incidence construction fails at its object definitions before signs or cancellation can be tested.
