## Specific decisions

- Barnette1969 attribution corrected to secondary evidence in the published Alt et al. bibliography; exact original-page wording remains unresolved.
- Anonymous2026 has no author; title and date are printed on the supplied manuscript.
- OpenAI2026b is pinned to the full upstream commit confirmed locally.
- Gorsky2023 retains journal year 2023 and arXiv v3 numbering.
- Feder2006 retains the ECCC report year 2006; its scanned manuscript is dated 20 January 2004. The 2008 author revision is distinct.
- Source PDFs preserve Tomas Feder, Tamas Kalman, Andras Juhasz and Frantisek Kardos with their printed diacritics; canonical entries retain the existing Unicode/TeX accent spelling. Terminal mojibake in PowerShell output was not treated as file corruption.
- Georges1989 and Holton-McKay1988 were missing from the library and were imported from the main bibliography. Georges metadata was then matched to the Elsevier page https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0095895689900129 . The associated DOI is 10.1016/0095-8956(89)90012-9; the existing entry is identifiable without adding it.
- Nash-Williams metadata is corroborated by the publisher record https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1112/jlms/s1-39.1.12 . Original statement checked secondarily in Schrijver.
- Added the sign-free inline bibliography keys unchanged. SchrijverCourse and Schrijver2017 name the same course; SchrijverHandbook and SchrijverUndated name the same handbook chapter. These aliases are intentional and documented in papers/README.md.
