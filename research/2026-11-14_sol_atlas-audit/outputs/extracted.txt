An atlas of proof routes for Barnette's conjecture
Readings of one proof, each drawn on a computed example. The proof is the one in the OpenAI paper:
every cubic, bipartite, 3-connected planar graph G has a Hamiltonian cycle. Most pages use one trian-
gulation T with 13 vertices, the dual of a graph G with 22 vertices. Black faces of T are grey, the outer
black face is the grey collar, and the three roots are hollow. A state is a set of filled corner wedges, one
in each black face. Page 1 is the map. The last page lists where each picture and number comes from.
