## Greyscale and deuteranopia

All 31 pages have a 120 dpi RGB, greyscale and full-severity linear-sRGB deuteranopia render. Contact sheets cover indices 00–30 in eight sheets per mode. The matrix and transfer curve encodings keep explicit signs, labels or marker shapes. Outlined versus filled wedges, hollow roots, disks versus squares, hatching, and the chart's solid versus dashed ranges retain their meaning. I found no categorical meaning carried solely by a hue and wholly lost in both simulations.

Visual weaknesses, not mathematical refutations: page 1's tiny green white tree, pages 8(b,c), 19(a,b) and 21(a,c) have thin white-tree curves that approach the grey background in greyscale. The dash-dot pattern remains but requires deliberate tracing. Page 3's blue tinted disk loses its tint contrast; boundary arrows and hatching still identify the disk. Pages 4 and 14 have wedges and hatching too small for contact-sheet reading. Page 18 has near-touching half labels, which neither colour transform fixes. Page 24(b)'s edge counts are crowded. Page 25's lower root-joining paths need stronger dashed emphasis. These are print-legibility defects; calling them complete colour-encoding failures would overstate the inspection.

Exact local fixes are in EDITS.md. Use the full PNGs, not only contact sheets, for label placement. The report does not certify every possible printer, reduction scale, or colour-vision phenotype.
