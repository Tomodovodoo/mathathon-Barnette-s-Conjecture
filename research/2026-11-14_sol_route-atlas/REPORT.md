# Atlas work log

The atlas will compare 26 readings of the supplied proof.
Every graph object will be computed by build.py using the read-only figure toolkit.
New calculations will be labelled COMPUTED and will not be promoted to general proofs.
Refereed reports take precedence over their submissions.
Human mathematical review remains pending.

## Scope

Owned outputs are digestions/atlas/ and this folder only. The build imports paper/figures/draw.py and model.py, reads the current running and octahedron JSON and saved layouts, and loads barnette-figs.sty through TEXINPUTS. The current toolkit uses running.json and layout-running.json, rather than the historical example.json and layout.json named in the brief.
