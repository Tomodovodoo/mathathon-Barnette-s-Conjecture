# Sources opened for the audit

The three project papers were read in full for OpenAI and in the specified relevant sections for Li and the constructive exposition, using their supplied layout text. No supplied paper was modified.

* OpenAI, *Paired states and Hamiltonian cycles in cubic bipartite planar graphs*, 24 September 2026, especially Sections 3, 5, 6 and 7.
* Zhentao Li, *A Note on Barnette*, supplied paper, Lemma 9, Theorem 10, recovery section and Appendix C.
* *A Constructive Proof of Barnette's Conjecture*, supplied 18 October 2026 manuscript, Theorem 1.2, Proposition 2.1, Section 3, Theorem 5.2, Lemma 7.1 and Section 7.3.
* Takanori Akiyama, Takao Nishizeki and Nobuji Saito, *NP-Completeness of the Hamiltonian Cycle Problem for Bipartite Graphs*, Journal of Information Processing 3(2), 1980, 73–76. Original author-hosted scan: https://www.ecei.tohoku.ac.jp/alg/nishizeki/sub/j/DVD/PDF_J/J029.pdf . The scan was downloaded locally and its first page rendered and inspected. Section 2 is the planar 2-connected cubic bipartite result needed in the reduction. The separately stated 3-connected class has no planarity restriction.
* Stefan Felsner, *Lattice Structures from Planar Graphs*, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 11(1), 2004, R15, DOI 10.37236/1768. Full paper opened: https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v11i1r15/pdf/ . Theorem 1 and the rigid-edge/essential-cycle discussion were checked.
* Maximilian Gorsky, Raphael Steiner and Sebastian Wiederrecht, *Matching Theory and Barnette's Conjecture*, arXiv:2202.11641v3, 15 August 2022, published Discrete Mathematics 346, 2023, 113249. Full primary HTML opened: https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11641v3 . Theorems 4.4 and 4.16 were checked for the exact P4 and Pfaffian implications.

The author reports also mention minimum-order non-Hamiltonian graphs and a cofacial Kelmans result. This audit does not claim to have opened their original proofs or independently established those historical minimum-order assertions. None is used as an input to the integrated structural statements.

Local downloaded source PDFs and reading renders are scratch and excluded from Git. The exact primary URLs are sufficient to recover them.
