Proposition 3.9. If s is a good state, then Ps is an acyclic selection containing e0. Consequently, if T
has a good state for t0 and e0, then G has a Hamiltonian cycle avoiding the edge that crosses e0.
Proof. By Lemma 3.6, Ps is a selection containing e0. Let P0 = Ps \ {e0} and direct the side of each
t ∈A in P0 out of s(t), which is one of its ends. These are chosen sides for the state s.
Suppose P0 contains a cycle Z. By Lemma 3.7 it is a directed cycle of chosen sides, so the disk identity,
Lemma 3.8, gives W −B = 1 and h −2ℓ= 3. Now count the faces inside Z through Ps, which matches
every face with the face across its selected side. The selected side of a face inside Z lies in the closed
disk; it is not e0, whose ends are roots. If it is strictly inside Z, it matches the face with another face
inside Z of the other colour. Every edge of Z lies in Ps, so it is the selected side of both its faces, one
inside and one outside. Thus exactly ℓblack faces and h −ℓwhite faces inside Z are matched across Z,
and the others are matched in pairs inside. Hence B −ℓ= W −(h −ℓ), that is W −B = h −2ℓ= 3,
contradicting W −B = 1.
So P0 is a forest, and by Lemma 3.7 each component contains exactly one root. The edge e0 =
ab joins the components of a and b, so Ps = P0 ∪{e0} is acyclic.

4. Pairs of states
We do not know how to point to a good state. This section shows that one exists, by a count in four
steps. A second corner choice turns goodness into the absence of cycles (Lemma 4.2). Every pair of
choices gets a sign, and the pairs that fail cancel in twos (Lemma 4.5). A weight, built from the white
faces that certain cycles enclose, lets the same sum be computed in a second way (Lemma 4.6). The
second computation shows that the sum is not zero (Proposition 4.8), so some pair does not fail.
Why two states. Goodness asks two things of Qs, no cycle and one root per component. The second
condition concerns whole components. Another state r that differs from s in every face reduces the second
condition to the first. It gives every vertex of X exactly one outgoing edge of Qs, and then a component
without a cycle contains exactly one root.
