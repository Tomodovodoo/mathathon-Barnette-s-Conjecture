6. Separating triangles
Let ∆= xyz be a separating triangle of a coloured triangulation T, and let D be one of the two closed
disks it bounds. The piece TD consists of the vertices, edges and faces of T in D, together with one new
face, the cap, bounded by ∆on the other side.
