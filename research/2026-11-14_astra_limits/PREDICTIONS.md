# Predictions recorded before experiments

All predictions are CONJECTURE, T5, unreviewed.

1. Non-bipartite inputs fail first at proper face coloring, already at K4; Tutte is needed only to refute Hamiltonicity. A maximum cut coloring leaves monochromatic adjacencies and breaks unique-black ownership of edges.
2. A theta of three copies of cube-minus-edge between two terminals is cubic bipartite planar and non-Hamiltonian. Its dual has parallel edges. Pair existence should fail for some roots. Minimality is not assumed.
3. On an orientable genus-g surface, |A|-|X|=2g. Torus states with the original three roots cannot exist. The 3-by-3 periodic triangular grid should give an explicit face-bicolored torus example.
4. All plantri Eulerian triangulations through 12 vertices should satisfy the exact black-inner separator criterion, with the first separating example at 9 vertices. Recursive recovery should avoid all three root-face edges, one at a time.
5. Tree counts should vary with root on asymmetric examples. Recovery should be injective but may not be surjective onto cycles avoiding the chosen edge. Enumerate cycles independently by complementing perfect matchings.
6. Zero spoke weights should give the zero polynomial already on the cube. Exactly one tree matching should be impossible by the sign balance in constructive Theorem 5.2; two on the cube is tight. Relaxed face choices should violate the disk identity on a small triangulation.
7. Single-edge inclusion and P4 should pass. Arbitrary include-one/avoid-another and bipartite Hamiltonian-connectedness may fail across small cuts. Search every edge pair and opposite-color endpoint pair on the small corpus.
