## What failed or was cut

- I first wrote two caption facts from memory of the data. One was wrong (which vertex starts the orbit in Figure 4). Both are now asserted by `check.py`.
- The Markdown copy is generated by a 70-line converter (`code/tex2md.py`), not written by hand. It keeps TeX math and replaces each figure by its caption.
- The verification of Theorems 6.5 and 6.8 uses all deleted-corner maps only for `n <= 7`; for 11 and 12 vertices it uses the covers and 20 random maps per instance.
- I did not replay the periodic walks at 38 vertices, the M-convexity failure, the non-transitivity of the `ι_t` at 13 and 14 vertices, or anything about the sister conjectures. I ran out of time, about ten minutes over the eighty.
- The first sister lemma (some degree-four vertex has a usable contraction) has no explicit verified range in the sources I read, so the note says only that no counterexample is known.
