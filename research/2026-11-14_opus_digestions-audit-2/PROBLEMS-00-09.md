# Problems found in 00-overview.md and 09-what-is-still-open.md

For their owner. I did not edit either file. Each item names the line or passage, says what is wrong, and suggests a fix. "Steps 01-08" means the documents as revised in this audit.

## 00-overview.md

1. **"Examples used throughout", running example row.** It calls the 13-vertex example the "smallest case with a cancelling pair and a cycle that encloses a vertex". Step 05 (and `research/2026-11-14_fable_route-combinatorics/DATA.md`) says the first state with both a partner and a cycle in $Q_s$ appears at 12 vertices. The conjunction with "a cycle that encloses a vertex" may rescue the claim, but nothing in the project checks it. Suggest: "a cancelling pair together with a cycle of $D$ that encloses vertices; the paper chose it for that".
2. **Sentence 9 and the diagram node "07 Spokes minus s is a spanning tree".** The paper and step 07 now go through $S_s = Q_s \cup \{ao, bo\}$, a spanning tree of $T$ (paper Lemma 3.5), and the tree of faces $Y_s = T^* - S_s^*$ (Lemma 3.6), rooted at $t_0$. Spokes are no longer needed. Suggest rewording sentence 9 as "The opposite sides of that state, with two sides of $t_0$, form a spanning tree of $T$. Dually the faces of $T$ form a tree, which matches every face with a neighbour; the shared sides are one side per face, and their complement in $G$ is a Hamiltonian cycle."
3. **"The exact value three is needed once, in step 06."** In the paper it is used twice: in the sign formula of Lemma 4.4 (hence in step 05 as well as 06) and in the recovery count of Proposition 3.9 ($W - B = 1$ against $3$). The diagram edge "G1 parity → N P is a forest" follows OpenAI's parity version; the paper's version uses the exact count. Step 04's usage table has been corrected; 00 should match it.
4. **"The documents" table** gives sections of the three source papers but not of the project's paper, which every step now cites first. Suggest a column "paper" with: 01 Intro and Prop. 2.6(1); 02 Section 2; 03 Lemma 3.1, Defs 3.2, 3.4, Lemmas 3.5, 4.2, Section 5; 04 Lemmas 3.7, 3.8; 05 Lemmas 4.3 to 4.5; 06 Lemmas 4.6, 4.7, Prop. 4.8; 07 Lemma 3.6, Prop. 3.9, Section 7; 08 Section 6 and Section 7.
5. **"All numbers were computed by `research/2026-11-14_fable_digestions/code/examples.py`".** Still true for the original numbers; this audit recomputed every running-example, cube and prism number with an independent script on the paper's orientation model (`research/2026-11-14_opus_digestions-audit-2/code/recompute.py`). Worth one sentence.
6. **"Where the project stands now"** uses undefined labels in a document meant for a reader who knows basic graph theory: "proof tier", "Rainbow Step R", "unique-factor completion", "Step X", "G and X, also called ML1 and ML2", "C3", "P2", "face-side packing", "simultaneous integrality". "Review changes the review state, never the proof tier" means nothing to that reader. Suggest one plain sentence per open door, pointing to 09 for the labels, or a link to the glossary, which now defines all of them.
7. **Sentence 4** defines a good state; step 03 now gives the paper's equivalent form ($S_s$ a spanning tree). Optional: mention it, since sentence 9 depends on it.
