# Novelty statement

## Suggested introduction text

LIT, with the historical inference qualified in the final sentence. The dual description of Hamiltonian cycles by two induced trees goes back to Stein's region partitions and is stated in graph form by Alt, Payne, Schmidt and Wood. Feder and Subi developed an earlier route through quasi spanning trees of faces after contraction of a facial colour class. Kelmans's strengthening and decomposition results already explain the role of a prescribed avoided edge and of cyclically four-edge-connected pieces. Tutte's trinity supplies the incidence states, and Berman's determinant formula already counts these states with a common determinant sign. The contribution of the supplied OpenAI proof lies in the additional forest-state existence argument: a disk identity ties the matching bijection to a signed boundary count; two different regroupings of disjoint state pairs cancel cyclic second states and prove that the total is nonzero through positive spoke circulations. To our knowledge, the pre-2026 sources inspected here do not contain this combination or its use to obtain Hamiltonicity without face-size restrictions.

Citations for that paragraph: Stein 1971, Theorem 2.2; APSW 2016, Lemma 5 and Section 5; Feder–Subi TR06-015, with the readable 2008 author revision, Section 2; Hertel 2005, Corollary 3; Tutte 1975 as developed in Hine–Kálmán 2018, Sections 1 and 4; Berman 1980 as stated in Kálmán 2013, Theorem 9.10; OpenAI Sections 3–7. Original-source limitations are in SOURCES.md.

## What should not be claimed

LIT. Neither two induced trees, prescribed-edge avoidance, separating-triangle gluing, incidence matchings called states, nor counting those states by a determinant is new. Complementary plane-tree duality is classical. Double-dimer regrouping of two matchings into independently orientable union cycles is also older; O explicitly cites Kenyon. Temperley/Kenyon–Propp–Wilson is related background rather than an invoked proof of the forest-state existence statement.

REFUTED. BF21 Theorem 11's even-interior condition is not O's black-inner obstruction under the standard contraction. The truncated-octahedron counterexample is proved in BRIDGES.md. Consequently that proposed identification cannot establish that the new argument is simply Feder–Subi's argument with a restriction removed.

PROVED-HERE. The direct prescribed-edge inclusion result needs only the avoidance construction and cubicity. It should be presented as a short corollary, not credited as a new strengthening theorem. The nonplanar Pfaffian conclusion likewise follows through an existing GSW equivalence and is already O Corollary 7.2.

HUNCH. "Adds one new idea" is too compressed for an introduction. The disk identity, its use in the fixed-second-state cancellation, and the compatible positive-weight/nonvanishing argument form the substantive existence mechanism. An older theorem giving one part would not automatically give the whole argument. Conversely, absence of a matching keyword in a finite literature search does not prove originality of every lemma.
