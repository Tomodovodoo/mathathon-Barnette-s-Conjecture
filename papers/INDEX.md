## The conjecture and its reformulations

| Paper | File | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S. K. Stein, *B-sets and planar maps*, Pacific J. Math. 37 (1971), 217–224. | `stein-1971-b-sets-and-planar-maps.pdf` | A Hamiltonian cycle is the same as a split of the dual into two induced trees; this is the target of the proof. |
| H. Alt, M. S. Payne, J. M. Schmidt, D. R. Wood, *Thoughts on Barnette's conjecture*, Australas. J. Combin. 64 (2016), 354–365. | `alt-payne-schmidt-wood-2016-thoughts-on-barnettes-conjecture.pdf` | The two-induced-trees form as used (Lemma 5), and the reduction to graphs without small cuts (Section 5). |
| A. Hertel, *A survey & strengthening of Barnette's conjecture*, University of Toronto, 2005. | `hertel-2005-survey-and-strengthening-of-barnettes-conjecture.pdf` | The older history, and the equivalent form with a prescribed edge (Corollary 3). |
| M. Gorsky, R. Steiner, S. Wiederrecht, *Matching theory and Barnette's conjecture*, Discrete Math. 346 (2023), 113249. arXiv:2202.11641v3. | `gorsky-steiner-wiederrecht-2023-matching-theory-and-barnettes-conjecture.pdf` | The prescribed-edge form (Theorem 4.3) and the consequence for Pfaffian graphs (Theorem 4.16). |
| C. Hine, T. Kálmán, *Clock theorems for triangulated surfaces*, arXiv:1808.06091, 2018. | `hine-kalman-2018-clock-theorems-for-triangulated-surfaces.pdf` | States as the corner choices of Tutte's trinity, and the moves between them. |
