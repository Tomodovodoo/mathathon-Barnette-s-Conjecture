# Papers

The three accounts of the proof and the background papers it rests on.

- [INDEX.md](INDEX.md) lists every PDF here with its citation and what it is used for.
- [barnette.bib](barnette.bib) is the bibliography for the paper.
- `text/` holds plain-text copies of the three accounts.

Files are named `author-year-short-title.pdf`. Add a paper only if the proof cites it or a reader needs it to follow the proof.
