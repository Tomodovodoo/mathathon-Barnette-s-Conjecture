# Synthesis figures

Both figures are single-page vector PDFs. Their PNGs were rendered from the PDFs with PyMuPDF and visually inspected.

- products.pdf and products.png show selected source and evidence relationships, not proof independence or a complete dependency graph. Mathematical dependencies are in [the blueprint](../../proofs/blueprint/README.md).
- route-outcomes.pdf and route-outcomes.png count the records in [routes.py](../route-map/routes.py). The category rules are editorial and exclusive; mathematical methods overlap. Proved includes unreviewed written proofs. Open includes speculative suggestions.

Run count.py, then figures.py in [the synthesis research folder](../../research/2026-11-14_astra_synthesis/). The per-route assignment ledger and count snapshot are there. Neither chart is a new mathematical result.
