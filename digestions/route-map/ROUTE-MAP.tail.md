## Shared steps and live alternatives

The supplied proofs share the disk identity, a sign-reversing cancellation with the second state fixed, and a nonzero lowest coefficient after regrouping by covers. The partner certifies root connectivity. These are statements about the supplied routes, not impossibility theorems for other proofs.

The failed shortcuts are kept as routes with lessons and certificates. Felsner's pair-lattice extremes fail at V=18; Propp's state-clock lattice already has an unpartnered extreme at V=10. R2, extreme or irreducible, fails at V=33. Natural two-matroid and integral-LP relaxations fail; exact reformulations alone supply no existence proof. The signed sum cannot be recovered from the ordinary rank polynomial of the factor lattice.

The contraction route has two missing lemmas, ML1 and ML2. C3 allows plateaus among pairs. Rainbow exchange X allows plateaus among white trees, while the surplus walk alone can cycle. Two-tree packing is proved and independently AI-refereed, but holds for every choice of deleted corners and so never sees the states; its face-side twin P2 and exact degree rounding are open. These are the current unsigned doors; their finite evidence is not a proof.

- The upstream Lean proof was built locally. The real-sign replacement main theorem has an independent rebuild and statement check.
