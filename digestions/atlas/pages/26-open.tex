The diagram separates three research questions from the established route through the forest step. First, can a local exchange procedure reach a tree matching with a proved efficient bound? Existing reachability statements and marked walks do not provide polynomial time. Second, do the two extremes of the pair alpha-orientation lattice jointly guarantee a tree orientation? Separate extreme guarantees are already false, so the disjunction needs its own proof or counterexample. Third, can contraction lift tree matchings through a controlled injection, or can the exponential lower bound in the minimum D-cycle count be proved? The family calculations provide tests, not proofs of these general claims. Floer terminology supplies another dictionary, but no independent nonvanishing theorem in the reviewed sources. Each door reconnects to a specific checked object in this atlas, so a proposed advance can say which missing implication it supplies.

\textbf{Status.} CONJECTURE and HUNCH only for the new implications. Unrefereed conjecture and induction reports; finite checks are not general guarantees. Established backbone is supplied literature.
