# 24. Earlier methods meet the new forest step

Stein's two-tree viewpoint already identifies the target: a partition of triangulation vertices into two induced trees. The running picture shows exactly that endpoint. Kelmans's strengthening explains why one asks to avoid a prescribed edge and why decomposition along small cuts matters. Feder and Subi work with quasi spanning trees of faces after a facial colour class is contracted. Those objects provide a different structural route toward the same Hamiltonian interface. The recent older-approaches report warns against identifying their hypotheses with the black-inner separator obstruction: an even-interior condition in one older theorem does not match it. The new paired-state step supplies the missing forest by cancellation and positivity, after an incidence-flow argument supplies pairs. Historical similarity therefore does not make the existence step redundant. This page compares endpoints and obligations; it does not draw an uncomputed Feder-Subi contraction on the running graph.
