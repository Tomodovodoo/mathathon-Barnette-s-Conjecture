An exposition of OpenAI’s proof of Barnette’s conjecture

The proof of Barnette’s conjecture in OpenAI’s release Paired states and Hamiltonian cycles in cubic bipartite planar graphs is simplified and visualized below, with different example graphs.

Example graph G

Names and numbers. Results are cited by the paper’s own numbers and section titles. The paper prints a name for only two of them: Lemma 4.1 (Disk identity) and Corollary 7.2 (Prescribed paths in Pfaffian graphs). “State”, “pair” and “root” are defined in its running text, not as numbered definitions.
